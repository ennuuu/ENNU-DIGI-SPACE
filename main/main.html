Hello, World!

My name is Pham Phuong Vy, also going by Anna or Ennu. I'm a Multidisciplinary Designer and Digital Media student currently studying at RMIT University based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.

Process

My creative skill set spans across 2D, 3D, and UI/UX, focusing in motion graphics, animation, video editing, and visual development.

Inquiry

For me, design is all about balance. Sometimes it’s wild, playful, and experimental, and other times it is neat, organized, precise. I like working with both sides, because I think creativity really happens when curiosity meets a bit of discipline. That mix shapes not just how I design, but also how I view the world.
